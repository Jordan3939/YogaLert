About Our Studio

A calm space to reconnect with yourself

Founded in 2014, Serene Yoga & Health began as a single mat in a converted garage and has grown into a full studio welcoming beginners, athletes, and lifelong yogis alike. We believe wellness isn't one-size-fits-all — so every class is taught with options for every body.

Our approach pairs classical yoga philosophy with practical health guidance: breathwork, mobility, and mindful movement you can actually carry into daily life.

  • Small class sizes for personal attention
  • Beginner-friendly & advanced tracks
  • In-studio and online session options

Why Practice With Us

Real health benefits, backed by consistency

Yoga is more than stretching — practiced regularly, it supports your body and mind in ways that compound over time.

Cardiovascular Health

Flow-based classes raise heart rate gently, supporting circulation and heart health over time.

Strength & Flexibility

Bodyweight postures build functional strength while improving range of motion in every joint.

Stress Relief

Breath-focused practice activates the nervous system's relaxation response, easing daily stress.

Better Sleep

Evening restorative classes calm the mind and body, helping students fall asleep faster.

Posture & Balance

Alignment-focused cues correct everyday posture habits and reduce common aches and pains.

Mental Clarity

Mindful movement and meditation sharpen focus and build long-term emotional resilience.
