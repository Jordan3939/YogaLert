Privacy Policy

How Serene Yoga & Health collects, uses, and protects your information.

Back to Home Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Serene Yoga & Health ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit this website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

We keep the information we collect to a minimum. This website does not currently use a contact form or collect email addresses. Depending on how you interact with the site, we may automatically collect:

  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, and pages visited, typically gathered via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information you volunteer if you call or visit our studio directly using the phone number or address listed on this site.

3. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Like most websites, we use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to understand how visitors use our site, remember preferences, and measure the performance of our content and advertising.

4. Google Advertising & the Google Display Network

This website may display advertisements served through the Google Display Network and other third-party advertising partners. As part of running these ads, Google and its partners use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • We do not control the cookies placed by Google or other third-party ad networks; please refer to the respective privacy policies of those networks for more information.

5. How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely to operate, maintain, and improve this website, understand aggregate visitor trends, and — where advertising is enabled — to measure and manage the performance of our ad campaigns. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Data Sharing

We may share limited, non-identifying technical data with service providers who help us operate this website and run advertising campaigns (including Google). These providers are only permitted to use such data to provide services to us or as required by law.

7. Your Choices

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Please note that disabling cookies may affect how parts of this website function. You may also manage ad personalization directly through Google's Ads Settings linked above.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date above indicates when this policy was last revised.
