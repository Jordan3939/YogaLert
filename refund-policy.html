Refund Policy

Our approach to cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds.

Back to Home Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Overview

We want every student to feel confident booking a class with Serene Yoga & Health. This Refund Policy explains how cancellations, rescheduling, and refund requests for classes, packages, and sessions are handled.

2. Class Cancellations by You

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before a scheduled class are eligible for a full refund or credit toward a future class, at your preference.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before class may be eligible for a studio credit, but are generally not eligible for a cash refund, except where required by law.
  • No-shows without prior notice are not eligible for a refund or credit.

3. Class Cancellations by Us

If Serene Yoga & Health cancels a scheduled class or session for any reason (instructor unavailability, facility issue, etc.), you will be offered a full refund or a credit toward a future class, whichever you prefer.

4. Class Packages & Memberships

Unused sessions in a multi-class package may be refunded on a pro-rated basis within 14 days of purchase, provided no more than one session has been attended. After 14 days, or once more than one session has been used, packages become non-refundable but may still be eligible for a credit toward future classes at our discretion.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a cancellation, rescheduling, or refund, please contact us directly by phone — we do not currently process these requests through an online form. Call +1 (000) 000-0000 (placeholder — add your number) or visit us in person at 123 Serenity Lane, Wellness District (placeholder — add your address), and our team will process eligible requests within 5–7 business days.

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned using the original payment method where possible and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your payment provider.

7. Exceptions

Promotional offers, trial classes, and gift purchases may carry their own specific refund terms, which will be communicated at the time of purchase and take precedence over the general policy above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy periodically. The version posted on this page at the time of your booking will apply to that transaction.

9. Contact Us

Questions about a refund or cancellation can be directed to us by phone at +1 (000) 000-0000 (placeholder — add your number) or at our studio address above.
